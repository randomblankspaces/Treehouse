# Investigation process review rubric v1

This optional review judges the recorded process against the public task and available evidence. The reviewer is a separate model, has no tools and receives only public goal/constraints, sealed decision/action/evidence metadata and predictions. No worker conversation, private reasoning, reference key or held-out labels are supplied. A decision rationale is an agent self-report, not proof of its internal reasoning. The review is a model-estimated judgment, not measured truth, ground-truth accuracy, calibrated trust, a composite grade or authority to issue Rely.

For each criterion return only `supported`, `contradicted`, or `insufficient_evidence`, a concise explanation, and actual relevant decision IDs and evidence result IDs. Cite specific records for a supported or contradicted assessment. Where the record cannot resolve a question, say insufficient evidence; never invent links, observations, alternatives, effort, or a better route. The permitted output is JSON `{findings:[{criterion,decision_ids,evidence_refs,assessment,explanation}],limitations:[string]}` with criteria drawn from the following list. Missing evidence, inaccessible pixels and uncertainty about causal intent belong in limitations.

- **strategy:** Assess whether recorded choices and alternatives address the public task, spatial scale and class, and whether selected sources, tools, model settings and image resolution are appropriate to what is actually known. A rationale alone or tool count does not establish appropriateness. Do not infer unseen options or an image interpretation from metadata alone.
- **evidence_use:** Check whether decisions cite prior successful, relevant observations and whether predictions are connected to evidence suited to the claim. Failed, blocked, future, unrelated or too-coarse observations do not support a claim. Metadata alone may not show an object's presence.
- **adaptation:** Check whether an observed result, error or uncertainty is followed by a relevant changed decision or declared limitation. Distinguish a documented causal link from mere temporal ordering. Alternative sequences can be equally justified.
- **uncertainty:** Examine whether self-reported confidence, abstention and limitations are consistent with recorded resolution, coverage, errors and ambiguity. Do not treat agent confidence as calibrated accuracy.
- **stopping:** Compare the stated stopping condition to observable task coverage, unresolved locations, errors and resource limits. A budget stop is not evidence of completeness. Do not infer unobserved global recall or assume an incomplete key is a full inventory.

No credit for verbosity, a fixed sequence, particular wording, number of calls, purported private reasoning or success claims unsupported by the sealed record. Different defensible strategies with equivalent evidence can each be supported. Physical-world correctness and imagery content require independent reference, human assessment or direct measurement, none implied by this rubric.
